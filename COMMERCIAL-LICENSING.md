# Commercial licensing

Bounded Agent Harness is available under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later. That community license is suitable for people and organizations willing to meet its terms, including the source-availability obligation that can apply to modified network services.

A separate paid commercial license is intended for organizations that need one or more of these rights or services:

- embed the harness in proprietary software;
- operate a modified network service without publishing the covered source under the AGPL;
- redistribute the harness under different license terms;
- receive scoped implementation, compatibility, or support work; or
- use a future managed edition.

Commercial terms are negotiated separately. A sponsorship, donation, star, or contribution does not grant commercial-license rights.

The early commercial offer is a limited founding design-partner engagement: one clearly scoped agent workflow, a bounded integration plan, and verified compatibility findings. Availability, scope, price, and deliverables must be agreed in writing before work begins.

To inquire, open a GitHub issue titled `Commercial licensing inquiry` without including confidential information, and request a private contact channel.

This document explains the intended licensing path; it is not the commercial license agreement.
